About ReceiptToCSV

ReceiptToCSV turns photos of receipts, invoices and statements into spreadsheets. It's a small, independent product.

Why it exists

Typing receipts into a spreadsheet is slow, and most expense tools want you to adopt a whole system to get there. ReceiptToCSV does one job: you add a receipt, you get rows you can paste, sort and send.

How it makes money

The free plan covers 20 scans a month. People who need more pay for Premium ($5 a month) or Team ($30 a month). That's the whole business: there are no ads, and we don't sell your data.

What happens to your receipts

Files are read by our service to pull out the numbers, and stored so you can come back to them. When you delete a receipt, it's deleted. The site uses analytics to see which pages are used, and a chat widget for support. The privacy policy has the details.

Talk to us

Messages from the contact page are read by the people who build ReceiptToCSV. Bug reports and feature ideas are welcome.
