How to use ReceiptToCSV

Everything from scanning your first receipt, invoice or statement to running a team. The free plan needs only an email.

Getting started

1. Choose a document type

On the Scan page, pick Receipt or invoice or Bank or card statement — see exactly what each covers below. This tells us which fields to read, so a statement's transactions and balances come back correctly instead of being forced into a receipt's shape.

2. Upload the document

Drag and drop a photo or PDF, or click to browse. JPG, PNG, and PDF are all supported — a printed page, a phone photo, or an emailed PDF all work the same way.

3. Let it process

Usually within 30 seconds, the fields are read for you — store/vendor/bank name, date, line items or transactions, tax, balances, and due dates where applicable. There's nothing to type — review the result and fix anything that needs a correction.

4. Export or save

Download the result as a CSV (for Excel, Google Sheets, or accounting software), a formatted PDF report, or view it later from your transaction history on the Transactions tab.

Document types

There are only two document types, split along one real distinction: does the document represent one transaction, or a ledger of many?

Receipt or invoice

Covers store receipts and invoices — an invoice is just a receipt with an optional due date. Extracted fields: store/vendor, date, line items, tax, total, payment method, and (for invoices) a due date. Defaults to expense; flip it to income if it's actually money coming in (see below).

Bank or card statement

Covers bank statements and credit card statements — one document, many transactions, plus an opening and closing balance. Each transaction comes back as its own line with a signed amount (negative for withdrawals/charges, positive for deposits/payments) — that sign already tells you whether each one was money in or out, no separate flag needed.

Money in and money out

Every Receipt-type entry is tagged expense or income. Scanned receipts default to expense — a generic photo can't reliably tell whether you were the buyer or the seller, so we don't guess. If it's actually income (a refund, an invoice you issued rather than received), edit the entry afterward and flip it. Manual entries (below) ask you directly, since you already know which one it is.

Adding a transaction with no receipt

Some transactions have nothing to photograph — a cash Facebook Marketplace purchase, a sale you made, an informal deal. On the Scan page, choose No receipt? Add it by hand and type in who you paid, the amount, and whether money went in or out. It doesn't use a scan.

Do I need an account?

Not on the free plan. You add an email so we can send you your results, and your scans are saved in the browser you used. If you clear that browser or switch devices, you won't see them there.

Premium and Team come with an account you can use on any device: choose Sign in, enter your email, and type the 6-digit code we send you. There's no password to remember.

Batch uploads (Premium and Team)

Instead of uploading receipts one at a time, Premium and Team accounts can select a whole batch at once — a month's worth of gas station and grocery receipts photographed in one sitting, for example — and get them all processed together.

  • Select multiple images/PDFs in one upload
  • Each receipt is processed independently, so one bad photo doesn't block the rest
  • Duplicate receipts are detected automatically and skipped

Organizing receipts with folders

On Premium and Team, the folder menu on the Transactions page keeps separate receipt histories for separate purposes — a "2026 taxes" folder, a "Client reimbursements" folder, a "Personal" folder — each with its own export settings and columns.

Asking the assistant (Premium)

Premium and Team accounts can ask plain-language questions about their receipt history directly in the app instead of scrolling through a table — for example:

"How much did I spend at Costco last month?"
"Give me a CSV of last month's receipts"
"What was my total grocery spending in Q1?"

The assistant can also make a fresh export — CSV, JSON, or PDF — on the spot, based on whatever you were just discussing. Available on both web and mobile.

Team accounts

Team plans share one scan quota across every invited member — there's no per-seat fee, and membership isn't capped. The owner manages it from the Team page:

  • Invite and remove team members by email
  • See combined scan usage across the whole team, and buy additional scan credits if needed
  • Mark individual receipts as pending, approved, or rejected — a lightweight review workflow for bookkeeping

Using the mobile app

The iPhone and Android apps work like the website — scan with your phone's camera instead of uploading a photo separately, and everything (including the assistant and receipt approval status for Team accounts) stays in sync with the web app when you sign in with the same email.
