What's new

Changes to ReceiptToCSV, newest first.

September 2026

September 8, 2026

  • Statements & invoices

    ReceiptToCSV now scans more than receipts. Choose a document type before uploading: "Receipt (Single Transaction)" (covers store receipts and invoices) or "Bank Statement (Multiple Transactions)" (covers bank and credit card statements). "Receipts" is now "Transactions" throughout the app to reflect this.

  • Income vs. expense tracking

    Every entry is now tagged as an expense or income. Scanned receipts default to expense (editable after the fact); statement transactions already carry a signed amount, so income vs. expense is automatic there.

  • Manual entry

    Log a transaction with nothing to photograph — a cash Facebook Marketplace deal, for example — directly from the Scan tab. No AI involved, and it doesn't count against your scan quota.

  • Filter by document type

    The Transactions tab now has a filter for document type, so you can quickly narrow your history down to just receipts or just statements.

August 2026

August 2, 2026

  • AI assistant

    Premium

    Ask natural-language questions about your receipts right in the app — "how much did I spend at Costco last month?" — and get instant, accurate answers backed by your real receipt data. Available on both web and mobile.

  • On-Demand exports from the assistant

    Premium

    Ask the Assistant to export exactly what you were just discussing — "give me a CSV of last month's receipts" — as CSV, JSON, or PDF, generated fresh on the spot.

  • Receipt approval status

    Premium

    Team admins can now mark receipts as pending, approved, or rejected — a lightweight review workflow for keeping track of what's been checked. Purely a status tag: it never affects totals, exports, or quota.

  • Mobile app parity

    Premium

    The mobile app now includes the AI Assistant and read-only receipt status badges, matching the web experience.

  • "Delete all" for receipts

    Clear out every receipt currently shown (including any active filters) in one click, on both web and mobile, instead of selecting them one by one.

  • Bulk delete accuracy fix

    Fixed an issue where bulk-deleting line items from a receipt could leave that receipt's totals out of sync. Deleting multiple items now correctly recalculates the receipt total right away.

May 2026

May 23, 2026

  • Business / team tier (beta)

    Premium

    Organizations can now invite team members under a shared admin account. Admins get a dedicated portal to manage members, view all org receipts in one place, and push settings to everyone automatically.

  • Admin portal

    Premium

    A new Admin Portal is available for org admins. It shows an overview of the organization (total members, seat usage) and a Members tab for inviting and removing users.

  • Org-Wide receipt visibility

    Premium

    Admins can now see and manage all receipts across every team member from the main Receipts tab. A member filter chip makes it easy to drill into a specific person's receipts.

  • Member invitation flow

    Premium

    Admins can invite team members by email. Invited users receive a join link and are automatically added to the organization with managed access.

  • Settings propagation

    Premium

    When an org admin saves their settings, they are automatically synced to all org members — keeping the whole team on the same extraction and export configuration.

  • Login code (mobile)

    Premium

    Premium users can now sign in on mobile by entering a 6-digit code from their login email instead of tapping the magic link.

  • Export format selection

    When exporting receipts, you can now choose between a summary (one row per receipt) or line items (one row per item) format each time.

  • Filter persistence (mobile)

    The selected date filter on the receipts tab now persists when switching between tabs.

  • Export confirmation toast

    A confirmation message now appears after a successful CSV download or email export.

Mar 2026

Mar 7, 2026

  • Filtering capability

    Can now filter receipts by upload date or payment date and export the filtered set.

  • Spreadsheet cleanup

    Removal of noisy spreadsheet data and rename of certain fields. Legacy settings can be toggled on/off if preferred.

  • Local date display

    Receipt dates now display according to the user browser rather than YYYY-MM-DD format.

  • Pre-Tax cost determination

    Improvements in logic to determine pre-tax cost and prevent cases where pre-tax equals total cost.

  • Project separation

    Premium

    Receipts can now be separated by project. Each project has its own separate receipt storage, settings, and exports.

  • PDF export

    Premium

    Receipts can now be exported in PDF format in addition to CSV/Excel.

  • JSON export

    Premium

    Receipts can now be exported in JSON format in addition to CSV/Excel.

Jan 2026

Jan 11, 2026

  • Itemized export toggle

    By default, only the totalized sheet (1 receipt -> 1 row) will be exported. Itemized (1 receipt item -> 1 row) can be enabled instead.

  • PDF support (mobile)

    The mobile app can now accept receipts in PDF format.

  • Itemized sheet expansion

    Itemized sheets (1 receipt item -> 1 row) now includes receipt summary information as part of each row.

  • Removal/Rework of "no items mode"

    This setting has now been named "Quick Scan". If enabled, receipts scan faster by skipping itemized line-by-line extraction. Disabled by default.

  • Mobile app extra fields fix

    Premium

    Custom extracted receipt data is now displayable and editable in the receipt detail tab.

  • iOS country override fix

    Country override can now be properly selected in the IOS app.

  • iOS keyboard dismiss fix

    Keyboard popup during receipt edit(s) can now be dismissed by tapping outside the keyboard.

  • App version number

    The "More Tab" now displays the app version number.

Dec 2025

December 15, 2025

  • Batch processing

    Premium

    Premium users can now upload and process multiple receipts at once.

  • Custom field extraction

    Premium

    Premium users can extract additional custom fields from receipts.

  • Login capability

    Premium

    Premium users can now login and access receipts from anywhere rather than being tied to a browser session.

  • Export rework

    We have cleaned up the receipt layouts and made it easier to export either all your receipts or a subset of them.

  • PDF support (web)

    Receipts in PDF format can now be uploaded and processed.

  • Country override

    Enabling this field will override any AI attempt(s) to deduce tax, currency, and country information.

  • No-Items mode

    Enabling this field will prevent line-by-line item extraction, thus improving scanning speed.

  • Email notifications

    Tired of getting email spammed? Now you can disable the automated email on receipt scan.

  • Filename customization

    Downloadable or emailed spreadsheet filenames can now be modified. A date can also be included in the name by adding the literal "<date>" to the filename.

  • Export to CSV and email
