From Paper Receipt to Spreadsheet Row

Most people trying to turn receipts into a spreadsheet end up doing it the hard way — squinting at faded thermal paper, typing totals into Excel by hand, and losing an evening to a shoebox full of gas station and grocery receipts. ReceiptToCSV replaces all of that with three steps.

1. Capture

Snap a photo, upload a JPG/PNG, or drop in a PDF — printed or emailed receipts both work.

2. Extract

AI reads the store, date, every line item, tax, and total — and lays it out in spreadsheet columns automatically.

3. Download

Get a CSV file that opens directly in Excel or Google Sheets — or a formatted PDF report if that's what you need instead.
